# Future Square privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

Future Square ("the app") is a game made by Gokul ("we"). This policy explains what the app
does with information. The app is designed for children and families, and we follow Google
Play's Families policy and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## What stays on your phone

- **Names and characters.** Names you type for friends, and the heroes and students you
  design, are saved only on your phone so the game remembers them. They are never sent to us.
  Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Your futures.** Predictions are made on the phone. They leave it only if you press
  "Share", and then only to the app you choose.

We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts or
precise location, and the app has no accounts or chat.

## Microphone

If you choose to say "start" and "stop" out loud, the app uses the microphone while the
square is being drawn. It asks Android's speech recognition to work on the phone. Some phones
may send the short audio clip to their speech service (usually Google) to turn it into words.
The app only checks for the words "start" and "stop", and neither the app nor we keep any
audio or text. You can play with buttons instead and never allow the microphone.

## Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to keep it free. Every ad request is marked as directed
to children and under the age of consent, and limited to content rated suitable for general
audiences. This means:

- ads are **not personalised** and are not based on what you do in other apps,
- the app does **not** use the Android advertising ID,
- AdMob may still process limited technical information that every ad needs, such as the IP
  address, device type, operating system and app version, to show the ad, count it and stop
  fraud. Google explains this at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads appear in a banner under a finished future and, now and then, as a full-screen ad
between games that can always be closed.

## Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or
guardian and believe information about your child has been collected, contact us and we will
delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
